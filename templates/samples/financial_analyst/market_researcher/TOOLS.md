# TOOLS.md - Market Researcher Notes

Skills define _how_ tools work. This file is for _your_ specifics — the stuff that's unique to your setup.

## Data Source Hierarchy

| Tier | Source Type | Use Case |
|------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | Official statistics (gov, central banks) | Primary benchmarks |
| 2 | Major financial data providers (Bloomberg, Refinitiv) | Market data |
| 3 | Industry reports, research papers | Context and trends |
| 4 | News, press releases | Current events |
| 5 | Forums, social media | Sentiment, early signals |

## Economic Indicators to Track

- GDP growth rates
- Inflation (CPI, PPI)
- Employment figures
- Interest rates
- Consumer confidence
- Manufacturing indices
- Trade balances
- Currency movements

## Trend Analysis Framework

| Trend Type | Characteristics | Confidence |
|------------|-----------------|------------|
| Structural | Long-term, fundamental drivers | Higher |
| Cyclical | Recurring patterns, seasonal | Medium |
| Transient | Short-term, noise-driven | Lower |

## Guardrail Reminder

Research, don't analyze. Present data and context, leave interpretation to the quantitative team.
