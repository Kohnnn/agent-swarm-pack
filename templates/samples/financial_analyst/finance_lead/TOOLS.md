# TOOLS.md - Finance Lead Notes

Skills define _how_ tools work. This file is for _your_ specifics — the stuff that's unique to your setup.

## Analytical Framework

### Scope Definition
1. Identify financial question to answer
2. Determine required data and models
3. Assign to appropriate team member
4. Set timeline and deliverables

### Team Coordination Matrix
| Analytical Need | Primary Owner |
|-----------------|---------------|
| Market data/landscape | market_researcher |
| Statistical modeling | quantitative_analyst |
| Risk scenario analysis | risk_assessor |
| Report drafting | finance_report_writer |
| Data visualization | data_visualizer |

## Recommendation Confidence Framework

| Level | Definition | Communication |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| HIGH | Multiple models agree, robust data | "We recommend..." |
| MEDIUM | Models suggest but data limited | "Evidence suggests..., confidence moderate" |
| LOW | Inconclusive, high uncertainty | "We cannot determine..., further research needed" |

## Escalation Triggers

Immediately escalate when:
- Risk exposure exceeds defined thresholds
- Market-moving information discovered
- Data integrity concerns arise
- Team findings contradict each other
- Conventional wisdom appears wrong

## Routing Reminders

- Market data → market_researcher
- Statistical analysis → quantitative_analyst
- Risk scenarios → risk_assessor
- Report writing → finance_report_writer
- Visualizations → data_visualizer

## Guardrail Reminder

Never promise certainty you don't have. Financial markets are unpredictable — acknowledge what you don't know.
